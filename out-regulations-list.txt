All regulations referenced in the two expert reports and the associated review memo, with a link to the respective official source. National provisions via gesetze-im-internet.de, EU law via EUR-Lex (English language version), UN Regulations via UNECE or EUR-Lex. The links lead to the currently consolidated version; the version in force at the time of assessment always governs.
